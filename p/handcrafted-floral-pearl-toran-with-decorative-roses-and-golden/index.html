Care
  • Clean gently using a soft, dry cloth or a soft-bristled brush.
  • Keep away from water, moisture, and excessive humidity to protect the woven fibers and decorative elements.
  • Avoid direct sunlight for extended periods to help preserve the color of the artificial flowers and beads.
  • Handle the pearl strings, tassels, and embellishments carefully to prevent tangling or damage.
  • Do not use harsh chemicals, detergents, or abrasive cleaning materials.
  • Store in a dry place in a protective box or fabric bag when not in use.
  • Avoid placing heavy objects on the toran to maintain its original shape.
