Care
  • Clean gently with a soft, dry cloth to remove dust.
  • Avoid washing or immersing the product in water to protect the fabric flowers, pearls, and decorative embellishments.
  • Keep away from excessive moisture and humidity to maintain the golden finish.
  • Avoid direct sunlight for extended periods to preserve the vibrant pink color.
  • Do not use harsh chemicals, detergents, or abrasive cleaning materials.
  • Handle the lotus flowers and pearl embellishments carefully to prevent damage.
  • Store in a dry place inside a protective box when not in use.
  • If using with candles or oil lamps, keep the flame away from the fabric flowers, pearls, and other decorative materials. Never leave a burning diya unattended.
